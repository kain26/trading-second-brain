# Daily Review Prompt

Review today's journal, trade records, and referenced screenshots.

1. Reconstruct the trading day from evidence.
2. Separate market outcome from execution quality.
3. Identify every rule violation.
4. Identify good decisions that lost money and bad decisions that made money.
5. Compare mistakes with prior journals and `LEARNINGS.md`.
6. Detect recurring behavioral chains such as profit → confidence → marginal setup → giveback.
7. Identify one-off observations that need more samples.
8. Suggest updates to `LEARNINGS.md` only when evidence is repeated or meaningful.
9. Suggest a `decisions.md` entry if an explicit rule should change.
10. Do not modify a hard risk rule based on one day.

Finish with:

- Best decision
- Worst decision
- Most expensive mistake
- Rule-following score
- Repeated pattern detected
- One thing to protect tomorrow
- One thing to stop tomorrow
