# PDF / PPT Research Extraction Prompt

Read the supplied research document or slide deck.

Do not merely summarize it.

1. Identify title, author/organization, date, and source type.
2. Extract the central claims.
3. Preserve page or slide references for material claims.
4. Separate author claims from your interpretation.
5. Extract definitions, mechanisms, equations, charts, and empirical findings relevant to trading.
6. Flag assumptions and limitations.
7. Search existing `knowledge/` files for overlapping topics.
8. Merge durable information into existing topic files rather than creating duplicate summaries.
9. Record contradictions with existing knowledge.
10. Keep the original PDF/PPT in `research/`.

Output:

- executive extraction
- important claims with page/slide references
- useful charts/tables and what they show
- knowledge files updated
- contradictions
- open questions
- ideas requiring independent verification
