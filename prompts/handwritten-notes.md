# Handwritten Trading Notes Prompt

Process photographs or scans of handwritten trading notes.

1. Transcribe faithfully.
2. Preserve headings, arrows, relationships, and rough diagram meaning when visible.
3. Mark unreadable or ambiguous portions as `[uncertain: ...]`.
4. Do not silently correct a trading statement just because it appears wrong.
5. Separate the original transcription from AI interpretation.
6. Classify each idea as fact, observation, hypothesis, rule, question, or reminder.
7. Search existing topic files and merge into the correct knowledge/strategy file when appropriate.
8. Preserve a reference to the original image.
9. Never promote a handwritten idea directly into durable MEMORY without supporting evidence.
